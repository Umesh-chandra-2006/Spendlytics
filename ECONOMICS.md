# Economics & Monetization

## 1. Business Model
Spendlytics operates on a **Freemium SaaS** model designed to generate leads via a free utility, converting high-value targets into an Enterprise success-fee tier.

### The Enterprise Tier (Percentage of Savings)
- **Target:** Large organizations (100+ seats).
- **Pricing:** 15% of first-year annualized savings.
- **Example:** If we audit an enterprise paying $39/seat for 500 Copilot Enterprise licenses, and identify that 400 of those developers only need the $10 tier (or are already covered by Cursor), we orchestrate the downgrade. Annual savings = $139,200. Our 15% success fee = $20,880.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
1. **Developer Communities (Reddit/HN):** Organic viral posts "roasting stacks."
   - *Estimated CAC:* $0.
2. **Open-Source SEO:** Targeting keywords like "Cursor vs Copilot pricing 2026".
   - *Estimated CAC:* ~$5 (blended content creation cost over time).
3. **LinkedIn B2B Ads:** Targeting Engineering Managers / CTOs with the "Stop the AI Context Tax" hook.
   - *Estimated CPC:* $6.00.
   - *Landing Page Conversion to Free Audit:* 15%.
   - *Audit to Email Capture:* 20%.
   - *Email to Enterprise Demo:* 5%.
   - *Demo to Close:* 25%.
   - *Estimated CAC (Paid):* $6 / (0.15 * 0.20 * 0.05 * 0.25) = $16,000 per closed Enterprise deal.

## 3. The Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M in Annual Recurring Revenue within 18 months, what must be true?

Assuming an average Enterprise success fee of **$20,000** per closed deal:
- We need exactly **50 closed Enterprise deals** ($1M / $20k).
- At a 25% close rate on demos, we need **200 qualified demos**.
- At a 5% email-to-demo conversion rate, we need **4,000 captured CTO/VP Engineering emails**.
- At a 20% audit-to-email capture rate, we need **20,000 completed audits**.
- At a 15% landing page conversion rate, we need **133,333 unique visitors** over 18 months (~7,400 visitors/month).

### Summary of Funnel Math for $1M ARR:
| Stage | Metric | Target |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Top of Funnel** | Unique Visitors | 133,333 |
| **Engagement** | Completed Audits (15% conv) | 20,000 |
| **Lead Capture** | Captured Emails (20% conv) | 4,000 |

This $1M ARR milestone is highly achievable organically if the free audit engine becomes the defacto "calculators" used by engineering communities to settle tooling debates.
