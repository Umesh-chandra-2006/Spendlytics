# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

## 1. Target Audience
Our initial wedge is **small-to-medium development teams (3-20 seats)** and **freelance solo builders**. 
Based on our user interviews, this demographic actively overlaps subscriptions (e.g., Cursor + GitHub Copilot, or Claude Pro + ChatGPT Plus) as an "insurance policy" against rate limits. They have the autonomy to change their tooling stack instantly without enterprise procurement processes.

## 2. The Hook
"Stop paying the AI Context Tax." 
We aren't attacking AI spending; we are attacking the *fear-based* "insurance policy" spending. When a developer holds Copilot Enterprise and Cursor Business simultaneously, they are paying for rate-limit insurance, not intelligence. By positioning Spendlytics as a quick, 2-minute health check, we lower the barrier to entry. The value proposition is immediate and strictly financial: "Find out if your AI insurance policy is costing you 30% too much."

## 3. Distribution Channels
### A. Developer Communities (Reddit, Hacker News)
Developers actively debate pricing on subreddits like `r/ChatGPT`, `r/ClaudeAI`, and `r/webdev`. We will launch Spendlytics as a free public utility in these forums, framing it as an open-source audit engine built by developers, for developers. 

### B. "Roast My Stack" Social Campaigns
Create viral Twitter/X and LinkedIn content where we take anonymized, bloated SaaS stacks from our database and publicly "roast" them, showing the optimized stack beside it. 

### C. Open-Source SEO
By open-sourcing the `auditEngine.ts` ruleset and publishing the `PRICING_DATA.md` as a living repository of AI costs, we can capture organic search traffic for queries like "Claude Team vs Max tier" or "Copilot vs Cursor pricing."

## 4. Onboarding Flow
1. User lands on page, sees the hook.
2. User selects their current tools and enters spend without creating an account (frictionless).
3. The engine calculates savings instantly.
4. To view the *detailed AI-generated summary* and actionable downgrade instructions, the user is prompted to enter their email (Lead Capture).
