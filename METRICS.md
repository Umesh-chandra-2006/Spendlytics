# KPIs & Success Metrics

To ensure Spendlytics is actually delivering on its core value proposition (identifying wasted AI spend) while simultaneously growing as a sustainable business, we track the following metrics:

## 1. North Star Metric
**Total Annualized Dollars Saved**
*Why:* This is the purest measure of the value we inject into the ecosystem. If this number is going up, it means our audit engine's pricing logic is accurate and users are taking our downgrade advice. It directly correlates with user trust.

## 2. Core Acquisition & Conversion Metrics
- **Audit Completion Rate:** (Number of users who see the summary / Number of users who land on the page). *Target: > 40%*. Because the form is frictionless and requires no login, this should be high.
- **Lead Capture Conversion:** (Number of emails collected / Number of audits run). *Target: > 15%*. Measures the effectiveness of the "gate" blocking the AI-generated executive summary.

## 3. Product Efficacy Metrics
- **Average Redundancy Ratio:** The percentage of tools in a user's stack that the engine flags as overlapping or unnecessary. 
- **Action Taken Rate (Self-Reported):** Percentage of users who indicate they actually modified their subscriptions based on our audit results. 

## 4. Financial Metrics (Future)
- **Enterprise Deal Velocity:** How fast we can convert a high-value audit result (e.g., $50k+ in identified savings) into a signed success-fee contract.
